# Unquarantine

Unquarantine processes Finder selections that the user explicitly chooses to remove from quarantine and re-sign.

## Language

**Finder Handoff**:
A selected group of filesystem items passed from Finder to Unquarantine for processing.
_Avoid_: Transport request, URL payload

**Right-click Run**:
A single user-initiated attempt to remove quarantine and re-sign the existing items from one Finder Handoff.
_Avoid_: Job, batch
